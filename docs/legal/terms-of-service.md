## 3. Service Description

Theobase is a church administration platform for Seventh-day Adventist Conferences and their local churches. The Service includes:

- **Church Administration**: Manage church profiles, districts, and organizational hierarchy.
- **Membership Management**: Track member records, baptisms, transfers, households, and church positions.
- **Finance Management**: Record tithe and offerings via dual-custody batches, manage budgets, track expenses, and reconcile church accounts.
- **Attendance Tracking**: Record weekly attendance by category (Sabbath School, Church Service, Youth).
- **Reporting**: Generate monthly treasurer reports, quarterly business meeting reports, and Conference-level dashboards.
- **User Management**: Invite clerks, treasurers, pastors, and other officers with role-based permissions.
