# Flat per-church subscription pricing

Theobase is sold by Taia Tiniyara, LLC (a US for-profit) as a hosted SaaS: each Conference or Mission (the tenant, ADR-0008) pays a flat USD 3 per organized Church per month, covering every module — present and future — with hosting included. The church count is derived from the tenant's unit tree rather than self-reported, changes apply from the next invoice with no proration, and there are no tiers, discounts, or minimums; Companies, Groups, and Branches are not billable. Migration off ACMS/ACAS/eAdventist is a paid service, with a free self-serve import for simple cases.

Considered options: per-member pricing (rejected — penalizes growth and is unpredictable for a volunteer treasurer); per-module pricing (rejected — fragments the "everything in one place" value proposition, ADR-0006); tiered pricing (deferred until there is real per-conference cost data).

Consequences: revenue per conference is small, so the model only works at denominational scale; the price must stay affordable to the grassroots (ADR-0005, ADR-0002).
