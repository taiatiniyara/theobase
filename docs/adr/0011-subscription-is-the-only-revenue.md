# The subscription is the only revenue

Theobase's only revenue is the subscription fee (ADR-0010) plus one-time migration services. Although the platform records tithe, offerings, and remittances moving through a church, Theobase never takes a cut, fee, or float on that money. We chose this because the platform's mission is to serve the denomination's grassroots (ADR-0001) and any monetization of church funds would destroy the trust required for adoption while contradicting the posture of being a neutral, compliance-enforcing tool (ADR-0003).
