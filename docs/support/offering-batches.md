# Offering Batches and Dual Custody

## Overview

The SDA Church Manual requires that all church funds be counted by at least two people. Theobase enforces this through **dual-custody batches**: the Treasurer enters the batch, and the Assistant Treasurer (or another authorized second person) confirms it. Once both confirm, the batch locks and becomes immutable.

## The Dual-Custody Workflow

### Step 1: Treasurer Creates the Batch

1. From the church dashboard, navigate to **Finance → Batches**.
2. Click **New Batch**.
3. Select the date (typically the Sabbath when offerings were collected).
4. Add offerings one by one:
   - Select the **fund** (Tithe, Local Church Budget, or Sabbath School Offering).
   - Enter the **amount**.
   - Optionally associate the offering with a member.
   - Add a note if the envelope had a designation.
5. Review the batch totals at the bottom of the form.
6. Click **Submit for Review**.

At this point, the batch status is "Submitted" — it is visible but not yet posted to financial reports.

### Step 2: Assistant Treasurer Confirms

1. The Assistant Treasurer (or another user with batch-confirmation permissions) navigates to **Finance → Batches**.
2. Batches awaiting confirmation appear in the "Pending Confirmation" tab.
3. Click a batch to review its contents.
4. Verify that the amounts match the physical envelopes and counting sheet.
5. Click **Confirm Batch**.

The batch status changes to "Confirmed". At this point:

- The batch is **locked** — no one can edit or delete it.
- Tithe and Sabbath School Offering amounts are **automatically forwarded** to the Conference.
- Local Church Budget amounts are added to the church's available balance.

## What If There's a Mistake?

If the Assistant Treasurer finds an error:

1. Click **Reject Batch** with a note explaining the discrepancy.
2. The batch returns to the Treasurer with status "Rejected".
3. The Treasurer corrects the batch and resubmits.
4. The Assistant Treasurer reviews and confirms the corrected batch.

Rejection and resubmission are recorded in the audit trail.

## Viewing Confirmed Batches

Confirmed batches appear under **Finance → Batches → Confirmed**. You can view batch details but not modify them. The audit trail shows who created and confirmed each batch and when.

## Batch Reports

From the batch list, you can export a batch summary as CSV for your physical records. This is useful for matching against bank deposit slips.
