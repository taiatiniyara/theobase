# Tithe Reconciliation

## Overview

Reconciliation is the process of matching tithe amounts forwarded by churches (as recorded in Theobase) against what the Conference actually receives in its bank account. This ensures that all tithe recorded in the system has been properly transferred.

## How Forwarding Works

When an offering batch is dual-confirmed in Theobase:

1. Tithe amounts are **automatically forwarded** to the Conference — no separate action is needed by the local Treasurer.
2. The forwarded amount appears in the Conference's tithe ledger.
3. The local church still needs to physically deposit or transfer the funds to the Conference's bank account.

Reconciliation catches the gap between "recorded as forwarded" and "actually received."

## Reconciling Tithe

### Step 1: View Forwarded Tithe

1. From the Conference dashboard, navigate to **Reconciliation**.
2. You'll see a list of all churches with their forwarded tithe for the selected period.

### Step 2: Match Against Bank Deposits

For each church:

1. Compare the forwarded amount in Theobase against the bank statement.
2. If the amounts match, click **Mark as Received**.

The tithe status changes to "Received" and the receipt date is recorded.

### Step 3: Handle Discrepancies

If amounts don't match:

- **Forwarded > Received**: The church recorded tithe but hasn't fully transferred it. Contact the local church Treasurer to resolve.
- **Received > Forwarded**: A deposit arrived that exceeds what was recorded. The church may have forgotten to enter one or more batches. Ask them to create and confirm the missing batch.

The Reconciliation view highlights churches with discrepancies in red.

## Reconciliation Report

From the Reconciliation page, click **Report** to generate a reconciliation summary showing:

| Column     | Description                                  |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Church     | Church name                                  |
| Forwarded  | Total tithe forwarded this period            |
| Received   | Amount the Conference has marked as received |
| Difference | Forwarded - Received                         |
| Status     | Reconciled / Pending / Discrepancy           |

## Church-Level Reconciliation

Local church Treasurers can also reconcile their Local Church Budget bank account:

1. From the church dashboard, navigate to **Finance → Reconciliation**.
2. Compare the system-calculated closing balance against the bank statement.
3. Enter the bank balance and any outstanding items (un-deposited offerings, uncleared expenses).
4. The system calculates the difference. A zero or small difference (due to timing) is normal.

## Tips

- Reconcile tithe monthly — it catches discrepancies while the details are still fresh.
- The reconciliation report feeds into the Conference's quarterly financial statements.
- Churches with consistent large discrepancies may need training on the batch workflow.
