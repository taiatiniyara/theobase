# Getting Started with Theobase

## Overview

Theobase is a church administration platform for Seventh-day Adventist Conferences. It handles membership, finance, attendance, and reporting — from Conference level down to individual churches.

## Signing Up

1. Go to [theobase.app](https://theobase.app) and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your name, email address, and a password.
3. Enter your Conference name and a short code (e.g., `NCAC` for North Caribbean Conference). This code is used in URLs and identifiers throughout the platform.
4. Click **Create Account**.

## Email Verification

1. Check your inbox for a verification email from Theobase (check spam/junk if you don't see it).
2. Click the verification link. It's valid for 24 hours.
3. If the link expires, you can request a new one from the login page.

## First Login

1. Go to [theobase.app](https://theobase.app) and click **Log In**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. After login, your access token is valid for 15 minutes. If your session expires, the app will automatically attempt to refresh it using the refresh token (valid for 7 days). If both expire, you'll need to log in again.

## Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you land on your Conference dashboard, which shows:

- **Churches**: Count of organized churches, companies, and branches.
- **Membership**: Total members across all churches, with recent baptisms and transfers.
- **Tithe Summary**: Aggregate tithe received from churches this month.
- **Notifications**: Pending items needing your attention (transfer approvals, unconfirmed batches, etc.).

## Next Steps

Once your account is set up:

1. **Create your first church** — see the "Creating Your First Church" guide.
2. **Invite users** — add clerks, treasurers, and pastors to your Conference through User Management.
3. **Configure funds and expense categories** — set up the financial structure that your churches will use.

## Getting Help

If you encounter issues during setup, contact support@theobase.app. Include your Conference name and the steps you've taken so far.
