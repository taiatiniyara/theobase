# Managing Members

## Overview

The membership module tracks every baptized member across your Conference, including their status, household, positions, and activity history.

## Adding a Member

1. From the church detail page, navigate to **Members**.
2. Click **Add Member**.
3. Fill in the member details:
   - **Name**: First, middle (optional), and last name.
   - **Contact**: Address, phone, and email (optional).
   - **Date of Birth**.
   - **Joining Method**: Baptism by immersion or Profession of Faith.
   - **Baptism/Joining Date**.
   - **Previous Denomination**: Required if joining by Profession of Faith.
4. Assign to a **Household** (optional). You can select an existing household or create a new one.
5. Click **Save**.

## Editing Member Details

1. From the member list, click a member's name to open their profile.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Modify the fields you need to update.
4. Click **Save**.

All changes are recorded in the member's activity log and the audit trail.

## Assigning to a Household

Households group family members together for directories and reporting.

1. Open the member's profile and click the **Household** tab.
2. Select an existing household from the dropdown, or click **Create Household**.
3. If creating a new household, enter a name (typically the family surname, e.g., "Smith Household") and optionally designate a head of household.
4. Click **Save**.

Members can belong to only one household at a time. If you reassign a member to a different household, they are automatically removed from the previous one.

## Membership Statuses

- **Active**: Current member in good standing.
- **Transferred In/Out**: Member involved in an active transfer between churches.
- **Deceased**: Marked upon receiving notification of death.
- **Removed**: Formally removed from the church roll (requires church board vote).

## Removing a Member

Removal is a deliberate action for formal removal from the church roll — it is not for deleting accidental entries.

1. Open the member's profile.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. Select a reason (Death, Missing, or Apostasy).
4. Confirm the removal.

A removal record is permanent and appears on the activity log. It cannot be undone without creating a new record.
