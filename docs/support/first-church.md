# Creating Your First Church

## Overview

Churches are the core unit in Theobase. Each church belongs to your Conference and contains members, financial data, and attendance records.

## Church Types

Theobase supports three church types:

- **Organized Church**: A fully chartered congregation with its own treasury, bank account, membership roll, and church board. This is the standard type for established churches.
- **Company**: A group that hasn't yet met the requirements for full church status. Companies operate under direct Conference supervision but maintain their own treasury and membership.
- **Branch**: A sub-group operating under a parent Organized Church. Branches share the parent church's treasury but maintain a separate membership roll for tracking.

## Creating a Church

1. From your Conference dashboard, navigate to **Churches** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Church**.
3. Fill in the church details:
   - **Name**: The church's full name (e.g., "Bethlehem Seventh-day Adventist Church").
   - **Type**: Organized Church, Company, or Branch.
   - **District**: Assign to an existing district, or leave unassigned if districts aren't yet configured.
   - **Address**: The church's physical address.
   - **Founded**: The date the church was established.
4. If creating a Branch, select the parent Organized Church.
5. Click **Save**.

## Bulk Import

If you have many churches to add, use the bulk import:

1. From the Churches page, click **Import**.
2. Download the CSV template.
3. Fill in church names, types, and locations.
4. Upload the completed CSV.
5. Review the preview and confirm.

## After Creation

Once a church is created:

- Invite the church Clerk and Treasurer through **User Management**.
- The Treasurer can begin entering offering batches and expenses.
- The Clerk can add members and record attendance.
- Assign positions (Elder, Deacon, etc.) to members.

## Editing a Church

Click on any church to open its detail view, then click **Edit** to change its name, type, district, or location.

## Tips

- Use consistent naming conventions across your Conference for easier reporting.
- Assign churches to districts early — it powers the district pastor's dashboard.
- Companies can be upgraded to Organized Churches later without losing data.
