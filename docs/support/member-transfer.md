# Member Transfer

## Overview

When a member moves from one church to another, Theobase handles the transfer through a three-step workflow: initiate, approve, and accept. This mirrors the process outlined in the SDA Church Manual.

## The Transfer Workflow

### Step 1: Initiate Transfer (Sending Church Clerk)

1. From the sending church's member list, click the member to open their profile.
2. Click **Transfer**.
3. Select the **destination church** from the dropdown. Only churches within the same Conference are listed.
4. Optionally add a note (e.g., "Member relocating for work").
5. Click **Initiate Transfer**.

The member's status changes to "Transfer Pending" and the transfer appears in the Conference dashboard.

### Step 2: Approve Transfer (Conference Secretary)

1. The Conference Secretary navigates to **Transfers** from the Conference dashboard.
2. Pending transfers appear in the "Awaiting Approval" tab.
3. Click a transfer to review details (sending church, receiving church, member history).
4. Click **Approve** to move the transfer forward, or **Reject** with a reason if the transfer cannot proceed.

If approved, the member's status updates to "Transfer Approved" and the receiving church is notified.

### Step 3: Accept Transfer (Receiving Church Clerk)

1. The receiving church Clerk sees the pending transfer on their church dashboard and in notifications.
2. Review the member's record and confirm it was voted at a church business meeting.
3. Click **Accept Transfer**.

The member's church membership now changes to the receiving church, and their status returns to "Active". An entry is added to their activity log documenting the transfer.

## Override (Conference-Level)

If a transfer stalls or encounters issues, a Conference-level user with the Auditor role can override:

1. From the transfer detail, click **Override**.
2. Select the resolution (force approve, force cancel, or redirect to a different church).
3. Provide a reason.
4. Confirm.

Override actions are prominently logged in the audit trail.

## Transfer History

Every transfer appears in the sending and receiving churches' transfer logs and in the member's activity log. Transfers that expire after 6 months (never accepted by the receiving church) are automatically canceled and the member returns to the sending church roll.

## Tips

- Initiate transfers promptly when you learn a member has moved.
- The receiving church Clerk should accept transfers before the quarterly business meeting — the transfer appears in the quarterly report.
- If you need to transfer a member to a church in a different Conference, contact support@theobase.app.
