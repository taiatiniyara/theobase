# Forgot Password

## Overview

If you forget your Theobase password, you can reset it through the password reset flow. This requires access to the email address associated with your account.

## Resetting Your Password

1. Go to [theobase.app](https://theobase.app) and click **Log In**.
2. Below the login form, click **Forgot your password?**
3. Enter the email address associated with your Theobase account.
4. Click **Send Reset Link**.

## Check Your Email

1. Check your inbox for a password reset email from Theobase.
2. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your **spam or junk folder**.
3. The sender will be from the Theobase platform email address.
4. The reset link is valid for a limited time. If it expires, repeat the process to get a new link.

## Setting a New Password

1. Click the reset link in the email. This takes you to the password reset page.
2. Enter and confirm your new password.
3. Choose a strong password that you haven't used before.
4. Click **Reset Password**.

You'll be redirected to the login page. Log in with your email and new password.

## If the Reset Link Doesn't Work

- **Link expired**: Request a new reset link from the login page. Reset tokens are single-use and time-limited.
- **"Invalid token" error**: The link has already been used or has expired. Request a new one.
- **No email received**: Verify you entered the correct email address. If you still don't receive it, contact support@theobase.app.

## If You Can't Access Your Email

If you no longer have access to the email address on your account, contact support@theobase.app. A Conference administrator can update your account email after verifying your identity through your Conference.

## Security Notes

- Password reset tokens are **single-use**. Once you reset your password, the link cannot be reused.
- The reset email includes only the link — no passwords are ever sent in email.
- After resetting your password, all existing sessions are invalidated. You'll need to log in on all devices.
- If you receive a password reset email you didn't request, contact support — someone may be attempting to access your account.
