# Recording Attendance

## Overview

Theobase tracks weekly attendance by category for each church. This data feeds quarterly reports and Conference-level dashboards.

## Attendance Categories

Attendance is recorded in three categories:

- **Sabbath School**: Members and visitors attending Sabbath School classes (typically 9:30-10:30 AM on Saturday).
- **Church Service**: Members and visitors attending the main divine worship service (typically 11:00 AM on Saturday).
- **Youth**: Attendance at youth-specific programming (AYS, Pathfinders, Adventurers, etc.).

## How to Record Attendance

1. From the sidebar, navigate to your church and click **Attendance**.
2. The page defaults to the current Sabbath (Saturday). Use the date picker to select a past date if needed.
3. For each category, enter the attendance count:
   - Enter the number in the corresponding input field.
   - Each field accepts whole numbers only.
4. Click **Save Attendance**.

The system records who entered the data and when. This appears in the audit trail.

## Viewing Attendance History

1. From the Attendance page, you'll see a table of past attendance records.
2. Each row shows the date, counts per category, and who recorded it.
3. Click any row to see the full details.

## Attendance Statistics

Navigate to **Attendance → Stats** to see:

- Weekly trends (attendance over the past 12 weeks).
- Average attendance by category.
- Comparison with the same period last year (once a year of data is available).

## Tips

- Record attendance each Sabbath or the Monday following. Consistent entry makes quarterly reporting accurate.
- Be consistent: count everyone present in Church Service even if they also attended Sabbath School.
- Attendance numbers feed the Quarterly Business Meeting Report, so accuracy matters.
- Only the Clerk or users with attendance permissions can record and edit attendance.
- If you need to correct a past entry, click the row and edit the counts. The audit trail will show both the original and corrected values.
